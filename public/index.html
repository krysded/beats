How it works

Cues. When focus ends you hear a descending chime and the sound glides into your break mix. Back to work = rising chime and the driving beat returns.

Binaural beats need headphones: each ear gets a slightly different tone and your brain “hears” the difference (e.g. 192 Hz + 208 Hz → 16 Hz). The research is mixed, so treat it as an experiment — try a week with it on and a week off.

Bands: θ theta 4–8 · α alpha 8–13 (relaxed) · β beta 13–30 (alert) · γ gamma 30+.
